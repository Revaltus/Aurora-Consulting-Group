## LinkedIn

Four Visalia rentals at $1,900 a month each can leave you with a tax return showing a small loss and a bank account showing $15,800. Both numbers are correct. Only one of them pays the November property tax bill.

That gap catches real estate investors every year. Depreciation lowers taxable income without costing a dime, while mortgage principal, a $9,000 roof, and an $8,400 property tax installment leave your account in full. One five-week vacancy plus turnover runs about $3,700, close to a quarter of that surplus.

The fix is a routine, not a spreadsheet overhaul: a monthly cash review after reconciliation, a 13-week forecast updated every month, and a quarterly tax projection before each estimated payment date. If you hold properties in separate LLCs or across states, each entity needs its own cash position and its own due-date calendar.
