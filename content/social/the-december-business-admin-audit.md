## LinkedIn

A property management agreement that auto-renews at 8 percent of collected rent costs $11,520 a year on a portfolio collecting $12,000 a month. Nobody sends a reminder when it rolls over.

Most Visalia investors can recite their cap rates. Ask when the umbrella policy renews, or which month the second LLC owes its annual report, and there is a pause. That pause is where money leaks.

Our December checklist covers the admin side of year-end: declarations pages side by side, dwelling limits against current rebuild cost, rent loss coverage, loan balloon dates, and every franchise tax and Statement of Information deadline. Two California LLCs means at least $1,600 in minimum franchise tax before a return is filed. Add a Texas rental in a third entity and there is another registration, another report, another date.

Do it now, while agents have time to quote and before 1099s and January 31 pile up. Fees are generally deductible. Late penalties are not.
