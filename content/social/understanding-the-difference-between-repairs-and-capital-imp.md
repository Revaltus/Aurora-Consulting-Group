## LinkedIn

A tenant's AC dies in July. Two weeks and $9,800 later, you have a new system and an April question: deduct it now or depreciate it over 27.5 years?

At a combined federal and California rate of about 35 percent, expensing that invoice saves roughly $3,430 this year. Capitalizing it produces a deduction of about $356 a year, worth around $125 in annual tax savings. Same invoice, same duplex.

The IRS sorts spending with three tests: betterment, adaptation, and restoration. Three safe harbors settle most of the small stuff. One of them, the small taxpayer safe harbor for buildings, lets you deduct all repair and improvement spending on a building up to the lesser of $10,000 or 2 percent of its unadjusted basis. Many Visalia landlords qualify and never use it.

Most investors make these calls invoice by invoice, after the work is done. We wrote a framework you can run before the contractor bills you, with worked examples from a Central Valley rental portfolio.
