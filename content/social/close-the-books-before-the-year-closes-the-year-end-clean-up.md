## LinkedIn

A $14,000 roof coded to repairs. A mortgage payment booked entirely as interest. A Venmo payment to a handyman nobody can identify.

That is what a preparer finds in January when a Visalia rental owner's file holds eleven months of uncategorized transactions. The hours go to detective work, and you pay preparation rates for it.

Our year-end checklist starts with the cheapest fix in Q4: reconcile every bank, credit card, HELOC, and mortgage account to its December statement. Then move personal spending into owner draws, split each mortgage payment into interest, principal, and escrow, and collect a W-9 from every vendor. The repair-versus-improvement line matters most. Repairs are deductible this year, while improvements are depreciated over 27.5 years on residential rentals.

Clean books let your preparer skip the rebuild, and that is what lowers the fee. It also leaves room for real planning while December 31 is still ahead of you.
