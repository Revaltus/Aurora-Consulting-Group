## LinkedIn

Nine occupied doors in Visalia, and the owner still feels short on cash. Usually it is two or three tenants and four or five vendor contracts eating what the other doors earn.

Here is the math from our Valentine's margin check. Door A collects its full $22,200 and takes 10 hours of your time, so it keeps 91% of scheduled rent. Door B is scheduled at $20,400, collects $17,000, triggers $4,200 in repairs, and eats 60 hours at $75 an hour. It keeps 41%. On a bank statement, Door B looks fine.

Before anyone sends a notice, test new terms: a $75 to $100 monthly increase, autopay, a repair portal. On the vendor side, asking your property manager for 8% instead of 10% on $199,800 of annual rent is worth about $4,000. California exits need a landlord-tenant attorney. The tax side, like unpaid rent and cash-for-keys payments, is where our CPAs and Enrolled Agents come in.
