## LinkedIn

A Visalia consulting firm spends $320,000 building out 4,000 square feet it does not own. The bookkeeper capitalizes all of it as one leasehold improvement. About $290,000 of that may have qualified for bonus depreciation.

In our latest post, we split that build-out: $85,000 in 5- and 7-year property (cabinetry, data cabling, built-ins), $205,000 in qualified improvement property (interior walls, ceilings, flooring), and $30,000 in 39-year structural work that stays slow. Tenants who pay for improvements can depreciate them. The classification is where the money is.

If you run a practice, a consulting firm, or a software company out of leased space, your lease terms, income, California conformity, and acquisition date all shape the answer. Under roughly $250,000, itemized invoices and a CPA's review often capture most of the value.
