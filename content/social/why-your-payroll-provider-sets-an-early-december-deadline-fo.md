## LinkedIn

An $8,000 bonus you meant to pay in December, but that lands in a January pay period, becomes next year's wage. It is one of four year-end payroll misses we see with Visalia real estate investors.

That early-December notice from your payroll provider is the window it needs to deposit taxes, reconcile Form 941 and California's quarterly filings, and build W-2s and 1099-NECs before the January 31 due date. Changes sent before the cutoff go through the normal workflow. Changes sent after become manual adjustments, and that is where W-2c corrections come from.

For investors with small payrolls, send in four things: bonuses (gross or net, paid by December 31), personal use of a company vehicle, S-corp owner health insurance, and employee data updates, including on-site manager housing.
